## Mentoring Philosophy

I believe research mentoring is a two-way collaboration. My approach focuses on:

- **Building research independence** through structured guidance early on, gradually transitioning to peer collaboration
- **Publication-driven projects** where mentees take ownership of papers from idea to submission
- **Career-aware mentoring** that supports both academic and industry trajectories
- **Open communication** with regular check-ins and clear feedback

If you are interested in collaborating or being mentored on research in computer vision, LLMs, reinforcement learning, calibration, or transportation AI, please reach out.
